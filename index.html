FAQ

Questions distributors ask.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your store link, or a spreadsheet export of your products. Supplier files help but aren't required.

Will this change my SKUs or prices?

No. Your SKUs and pricing stay exactly as they are. We improve the name, description, specs, category and photo.

How do you make sure the specs are right?

Every product is matched to its exact manufacturer part number, and every spec comes from the manufacturer's own page or spec sheet. Anything we can't confirm is flagged for you, never guessed.

What store platforms do you work with?

We deliver a ready-to-import file for Shopify, and a clean spreadsheet that works with BigCommerce, WooCommerce and most ERP and PIM systems.

Where do the photos come from?

From the manufacturer. If you already receive image files from your suppliers, we match those to each product too.

Do I need to get on a call?

No. Everything can be handled by email. If you'd like to talk, we're happy to.

Want to see your own products cleaned up?

Email your store link to michael@partpolish.com and I'll send back 10 finished listings, free. Usually within two business days.
